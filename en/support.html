Frequently asked questions

Does KanToDo cost anything?
KanToDo is free and funded through unobtrusive ads. With "KanToDo Premium" — a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription — you remove the ads permanently and additionally unlock iCloud sync.

Are my tasks synced?
With KanToDo Premium: the one-time in-app purchase unlocks iCloud sync. Once you enable it in the settings, your tasks become available on iPhone, iPad and Mac under the same Apple ID. Without Premium your tasks stay local on each device.

Do I need an account?
No. KanToDo works without registration. There is no separate login.
