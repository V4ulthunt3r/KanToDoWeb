Frequently asked questions

Does KanToDo cost anything?
KanToDo is free. The app is funded through unobtrusive ads — you can remove them permanently with a one-time in-app purchase.

Are my tasks synced?
Optionally: enable iCloud sync in the settings and your tasks become available on iPhone, iPad and Mac under the same Apple ID.

Do I need an account?
No. KanToDo works without registration. There is no separate login.
